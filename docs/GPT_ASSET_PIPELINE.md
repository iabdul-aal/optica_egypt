# Optica Egypt Local Section — Modern Flat Rebrand & Combination Mark Pipeline

> **Design Direction & Corporate Rebrand Standards:**
> Inspired by the modern flat redesigns of global industry leaders (e.g., **BMW 2020 rebrand**, **Mastercard**, **Apple**, **Figma**, **CERN**):
> 
> 1. **Logo Typology — The Combination Mark:**
>    - Optica Egypt is engineered as a **Combination Mark** (Wordmark paired side-by-side with an Abstract/Pictorial Mark).
>    - It is **NOT** an enclosed skeuomorphic "Emblem Logo" (like Starbucks or an athletic crest) where text and graphics are crammed inside a dense badge.
>    - The **Pictorial Mark** can stand completely on its own (as a favicon, app icon, social avatar, or physical pin).
>    - In the full logo, the Pictorial Mark sits cleanly **beside** the wordmark separated by balanced negative space.
> 
> 2. **Skeuomorphic → Flat Vector Transition (The BMW Standard):**
>    - **ABSOLUTELY ZERO GRADIENTS:** No linear gradients, no radial glows, no metallic chrome reflections, no lighting flares.
>    - **ABSOLUTELY ZERO 3D BEVELS / SHADOWS:** No embossing, no extruded stone textures, no drop shadows.
>    - **Pure 2D Flat Vector Geometry:** Clean monoline and dual-weight vector paths, solid color planes, and generous breathing room (negative space).
> 
> 3. **Global Optica Brand Compliance:**
>    - **`OPTICA`** remains the clean, untouched, authoritative global wordmark.
>    - **`EGYPT LOCAL SECTION`** sits directly underneath in flat solid typography.
>    - The section's custom mark is placed **beside** the wordmark (never inside the letterforms).
> 
> 4. **Solid Color Palette:**
>    - Canvas Navy: Solid Flat Midnight Navy (`#09131F`)
>    - Canvas White: Solid Flat Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
>    - Brand Gold: Solid Flat Egyptian Royal Gold (`#D4AF37`)
>    - Text Primary: Solid Flat Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
>    - Text Secondary: Solid Flat Slate (`#94A3B8`)
