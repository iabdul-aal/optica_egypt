# Optica Egypt Local Section — Master AI Asset Generation Pipeline (Single-Chat Workflow)

> **Design Philosophy & Brand Compliance Protocol:**
> 1. **Global Brand Integrity:** The word **`OPTICA`** is an immutable global trademark. It must NEVER be warped, distorted, or have hieroglyphs or objects placed directly inside its letterforms. It is rendered in clean, authoritative, geometric sans-serif letterforms with its signature subtle horizontal optical slit in the "O".
> 2. **Local Section Hierarchy:** Underneath the word `OPTICA`, the local section is identified with **`EGYPT`** (bold royal gold `#D4AF37`) and **`LOCAL SECTION`** (clean uppercase, tracked wide).
> 3. **The Egyptian Photonics Emblem:** Placed **directly beside the wordmark (to the right or left)**. It fuses ancient Egyptian heritage (the **Three Giza Pyramids**) with photonics philosophy (the **Eye of Horus / Wadjet**, serving as the optical aperture, photodetector, and lens of light).
> 4. **Visual Universe:** Egyptian midnight navy (`#09131F`), Egyptian royal gold (`#D4AF37` / `#D7AE5B`), laser cyan/teal (`#00ADEF`), and spectral dispersion across the desert horizon.
